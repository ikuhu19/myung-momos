OUR STORY

A Mother's Passion.
A Son's Promise.

"Every momo carries a piece of our family's journey."

In a quiet kitchen filled with the comforting aroma of steaming momos, a mother lovingly folded every dumpling by hand. What began as a simple passion for cooking soon became her way of sharing warmth with everyone around her.

Inspired by her dedication, her son turned that dream into Myung's Momos—bringing authentic Darjeeling flavours from the hills to every customer with love.
