OUR HERITAGE & HEART

A Mother’s Passion.
A Son’s Promise.

“Every momo carries a piece of our family’s journey and the true warmth of the hills.”

In a quiet home filled with the comforting aroma of steaming bamboo baskets, a mother lovingly folded every dumpling by hand. For her, rolling dough to sheer translucence and balancing Himalayan herbs wasn’t just cooking—it was her purest way of sharing warmth and nourishment with family.

Growing up on these flavors, her son made a heartfelt promise: never to compromise on genuine Darjeeling craftsmanship and to bring that authentic, comforting mountain taste down to the heart of Sagar, Madhya Pradesh.

That promise is Myung’s Momos. No artificial flavor enhancers, no thick factory-made skins. Just honest, hand-pleated momos made every evening with family devotion.

THE DARJEELING STANDARD

The Anatomy of a Perfect Momo

True Himalayan momos are defined by three indispensable pillars perfected over years of family practice.

01

Paper-Thin Wrapper

Hand-rolled dough that is delicate, soft, and never heavy. Rolled so fine it traps hot steam and succulent juices without ever feeling dense or rubbery.

02

Slow-Marinated Filling

Fresh vegetables and cottage cheese with traditional Himalayan ginger and herbs. Gently tossed to let every aromatic note shine through upon the very first bite.

03

Fiery Garlic Chutney

Made fresh with red chilies, garlic, and roasted tomatoes. Stone-ground in daily small batches to deliver that legendary hill-station kick and smoky richness.
