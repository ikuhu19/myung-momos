The Art of the Perfect Momo

We're obsessed with quality, and we believe you can taste the difference. When you eat at Myung's, you are experiencing our commitment to authenticity and craft.

Always Hand-Folded

We don't use machines. Each momo is folded by hand with precision and care, ensuring the perfect texture and seal. It’s a labor of love, and it’s non-negotiable.

Market-Fresh Ingredients

Our fillings are made daily using fresh, locally sourced vegetables and high-quality meats. We believe the best flavor starts with the best ingredients.

The Secret is in the Spices

Our spice blends are a closely guarded family secret, a unique mix of Himalayan herbs and spices that give our momos their signature, unforgettable flavor.

That Signature Sauce

A momo is nothing without its achar (dipping sauce). Ours is made from scratch every morning, a fiery, tangy, and nutty companion that perfectly complements every bite.

A Mother’s Passion, A Son’s Promise

In a quiet kitchen filled with the comforting scent of steaming dumplings, a mother poured her heart into every fold of a momo. What began as a simple love for cooking soon became her way of sharing warmth with friends and neighbors.

Watching her, her son grew up not just tasting those delicate parcels of flavor, but learning the value of dedication and love. He saw her eyes light up when people smiled after the first bite, and he knew this was more than food—it was her dream.

Together, they decided to take that dream beyond their small kitchen. With his support and her timeless recipes, Myung's Momos was born—a place where every plate carries a story of family, perseverance, and the magic that happens when passion meets love.
